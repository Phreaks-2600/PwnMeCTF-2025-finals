# IDY Roulette 2/2

## Description
The owner of an underground casino claims to have built the most unbeatable roulette machine ever made.
According to him, no one will ever hit the jackpot — the randomness is too chaotic, and the difficulty is through the roof.

Feeling untouchable, he even released the full firmware of the system.

But then he handed you a membership card...
…that doesn’t even hold enough credits to start a single game.

Now it’s up to you.
Find a way to play.
Beat the machine.
And claim what’s rightfully yours.
